# Research Notes

## Legal & Criminal Elements
- **Drug possession laws:** In the state where the story is set, what are the penalties? Felony? Minimum sentences?
- **Prison murder:** How are hits arranged? What are the risks? How would someone use poison to avoid detection?
- **Blackmail laws:** What recourse do victims have? But the couple cannot go to police without exposing themselves.
- **Blood type paternity:** How does blood type inheritance work? Which combinations would rule out paternity? (Example: If both parents are type O, child must be O; if father is A and mother is O, child could be A or O—need a scenario where child’s blood type is impossible.)

## Psychological Aspects
- **Trauma and repression:** How victims of sexual assault may block out the memory.
- **Post-assault pregnancy:** The psychological conflict of carrying the assaulter’s child.
- **Moral injury:** The psychological toll of committing (or orchestrating) murder.

## Setting Details
- Choose a specific state or city to ground the story.
- Research prison conditions and release procedures for hitmen.

## Timeline
- Outline the sequence of events:
  1. Early relationship, split, pregnancy.
  2. Return, raising the child.
  3. Discovery of assault / paternity.
  4. Planning and execution of the frame and prison hit.
  5. Years of peaceful life.
  6. Blackmailer’s release.
  7. Present conflict.
