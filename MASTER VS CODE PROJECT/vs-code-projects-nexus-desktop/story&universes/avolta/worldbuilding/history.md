# History of the Pact

## 1975 – The *Kimpasi* Vow
Agostinho Neto, the last *Nganga Mukulu*, gathers the *minkisi* of the 14 old kingdoms. At the *Mulemba* of Mbanza Kongo, he performs the *Havemos de Voltar* incantation, binding the spirits to the new nation.

The terms:
- Spirits lend their power to Angola.
- Every Angolan carries the *Kimbu* in their blood.
- Trained *Nganga* will maintain the pact.

## 1975–1990s – The Golden Age
Sorcerers advise the government. *Minkisi* guard borders, mines, and rivers. The *Soba* council ensures *Kixila* are respected.

## 1990s–2020s – The Fraying
Civil war kills *Nganga*. Oil and diamond wealth make the elite dismiss sorcery as superstition. *Minkisi* are looted or sold. The last trained *Nganga* age without successors.

## 2026 – The Awakening
The final *Nganga* dies in Huíla. The pact is not broken, but inherited: the *Kimbu* in every Angolan’s blood activates. Chaos ensues.

## The Future – The New Order
After the *Makisi* walk, the survivors forge a syncretic nation where sorcery is integrated into law, infrastructure, and daily life.
