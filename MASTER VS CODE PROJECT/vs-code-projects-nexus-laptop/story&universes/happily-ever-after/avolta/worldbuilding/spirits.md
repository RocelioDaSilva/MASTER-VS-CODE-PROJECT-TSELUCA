# Spirits & Entities

## *Makisi* – Masked Spirits of Justice
- **Cikunza (The Hunter):** A 12-foot masked figure with a slingshot. Fuses with military tech to hunt exploiters.
- **Pwo (The Ancestral Woman):** Weaves life force. She can erase a person from existence by weaving their *sanga* into a basket.

## *Kisimbi* – Water Spirits
- Guardian serpents and beings of the *Kwanza*, *Cunene*, *Cassai* rivers.
- They rise against polluters. Can “claim” the corrupt into an eternal underwater life.

## *Jijimu* – Giants of the *Mulemba*
- Ancient *Soba* kings bound to baobab trees. They walk the borders of old kingdoms, demanding *Kisua* (proverbs) as toll.

## *Kipalupalu* – Lost Souls
- Angolans who have no cultural connection. They wander the *mato* (bush), fading from memory.

## *Nkisi* – Power Objects
- Sacred carvings with reflective cavities. They are sentient and can merge with modern infrastructure (tanks, oil rigs, cell towers).
