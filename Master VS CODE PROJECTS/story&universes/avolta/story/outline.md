# Story Outline – *A Volta*

## Act I – The Dying Embers
- The last *Nganga* dies in Huíla, breaking the pact.
- Across Angola, the *Kimbu* in blood awakens: a *Kuduro* dancer in Luanda accidentally commands shadows; a grandmother in Benguela heals with a *Semba* rhythm.
- The *Makisi* begin to walk: a *Cikunza* mask fuses with a military tank, hunting corrupt executives.
- Three factions emerge: *Mukixi* (awakened), *Bakululu* (betrayers cursed), *Muxiluanda* (lost children fading).

## Act II – The Sorcerous War
- The *Kimbanda* academies reopen underground.
- The *Soba* council tries to negotiate with the *Makisi*.
- The diaspora begins to return, seeking the *Kudila* naming ritual.
- A geopolitical crisis: Angolan sorcery manifests in Lisbon, São Paulo.

## Act III – The New Order
- The *Nkisi* are integrated into infrastructure: oil rigs, internet cables, hospitals.
- A new constitution recognizes *Kixila* (taboo law) alongside civil law.
- The final confrontation: young *Mukixi* vs. old *Nganga* over the future of sorcery.
- The *Havemos de Voltar* is fulfilled: Angola becomes a spiritually sovereign nation.

## Themes
- Cultural reclamation as power
- The tension between tradition and innovation
- Identity, diaspora, and belonging
- Justice against exploitation (ecological, colonial, corporate)
