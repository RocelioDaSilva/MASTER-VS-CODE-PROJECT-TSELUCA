# Character Profiles

## Protagonists
### [Name] – The *Kuduro* Sorcerer
- **Role:** Young dancer in Luanda’s *musseque*.
- **Awakening:** Discovers that his *Kabetula* steps can bind shadows.
- **Arc:** From party dancer to one of the first *Mukixi* leaders. Wants a modern, syncretic sorcery.

### [Name] – The Grandmother
- **Role:** Elder in Benguela who remembers fragments of *Kisua*.
- **Awakening:** Her healing touch returns, but she must race to teach before she dies.
- **Arc:** Mentor figure; struggles to keep traditions alive against the chaos.

## Antagonists
### The *Bakululu* Minister
- **Role:** Former politician who sold *minkisi* to foreign collectors.
- **Curse:** His *Kimbu* curdled; he is now hunted by *Makisi* he once mocked.

### The Foreign CEO
- **Role:** Head of an oil company that ignored *Kisimbi* taboos.
- **Fate:** Merged with a *Nkondi* nail figure; now his own body impales him with nails for every barrel of stolen oil.

## Factions
- **Old *Nganga*:** Want strict adherence to tradition.
- **Young *Mukixi*:** Want to innovate with *Kuduro*, social media, and urban sorcery.
- **Diaspora Returnees:** Desperate to be “named” back into the land.
