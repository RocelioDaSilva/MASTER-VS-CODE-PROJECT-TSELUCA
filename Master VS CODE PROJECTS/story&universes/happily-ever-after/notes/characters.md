# Character Profiles

## Protagonists

### [Husband’s Name]
- **Role:** The devoted partner who sacrificed his career to return.
- **Conflict:** He discovered the child isn’t his and grappled with his rage before choosing revenge over abandonment.
- **Arc:** From romantic idealist to co-conspirator; now must decide how far he’ll go to protect his family from the blackmailer.

### [Wife’s Name]
- **Role:** The victim of assault who initially suppressed the trauma, hoping the child was her partner’s.
- **Conflict:** Guilt over not revealing the truth sooner; fear of losing everything.
- **Arc:** From passive survivor to active participant in revenge; now struggles with the weight of the lie.

## Antagonists

### The Assaulter (Deceased)
- **Role:** The best friend who committed the assault.
- **Fate:** Framed and killed in prison by the couple’s arrangement.

### The Hitman / Blackmailer
- **Role:** The death row inmate (or long-term prisoner) who carried out the murder in exchange for money/favors.
- **Traits:** Smart, patient, ruthless. He used poison to avoid detection.
- **Goal:** Extract money from the couple now that he’s free; he has nothing to lose and connections to enforce his demands.

### Corrupt Cop
- **Role:** The officer paid to arrest the assaulter.
- **Risk:** Could become a loose end.

## Supporting
- The Child (now older, unaware of the truth)
- Friends, family who suspect nothing
- Prison contacts of the blackmailer
