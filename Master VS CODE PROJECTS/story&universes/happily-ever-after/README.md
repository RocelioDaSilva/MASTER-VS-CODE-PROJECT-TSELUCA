# Happily Ever After

A drama exploring the cracks beneath a seemingly perfect family. When a couple sacrifices everything to build the American Dream, the buried truth of their past—a sexual assault, a secret child, and a murder—threatens to unravel their lives.

- **Logline:** After years of building a perfect life, a couple must confront the dark secret they buried to protect their family, now that the man they framed and had killed in prison is out and blackmailing them.
- **Central Question:** How far will they go to preserve their "happily ever after," and what happens when the past refuses to stay buried?
