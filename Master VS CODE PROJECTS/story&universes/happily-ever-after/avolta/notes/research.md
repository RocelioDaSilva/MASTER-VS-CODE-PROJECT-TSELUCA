# Cultural Research Notes

- **Kimbanda:** Traditional healer/sorcerer in Bantu cosmology. Works with *nKisi* (spiritual objects) and communicates with ancestors.
- **Kixila:** Taboos or ritual prohibitions that maintain balance.
- **Kisua:** Proverbs used in divination and ritual contexts.
- **Mulemba:** Baobab tree; sacred meeting place.
- **Makisi:** Masked spirits of the Chokwe and related cultures; they dance and also judge.
- **Semba / Kuduro:** Angolan music/dance forms. Semba has traditional roots; Kuduro is an electronic evolution.
- **Mussoco:** Offerings to ancestors (usually food, drink).

Sources to explore:
- Oral traditions from Bakongo, Ovimbundu, Chokwe, Ambundu.
- *The Kongo Nkisi* by Wyatt MacGaffey.
- Angolan literature: Agostinho Neto, Pepetela, Ondjaki.
- Modern *Kuduro* culture and its spiritual undertones.
