# Sorcery in *A Volta*

## The *Kimbu* – Blood Inheritance
Every Angolan carries the *Kimbu*, a spiritual potential awakened by the 1975 pact. Without training, it manifests erratically. With discipline, one becomes a *Nganga* (sorcerer).

## Schools of *Kimbanda*
- **Lemba:** Water & ancestor magic. Negotiates with *Kisimbi* (water spirits).
- **Kimpasi:** Healing & curse-breaking. The art of managing spiritual illness.
- **Nkisi:** Object sorcery. Binding spirits into *minkisi* (power objects) that fuse with modern tech.
- **Kisua:** Proverbial incantations. Every old saying is a spell.

## Sorcerous Technology
- **Ngoma Drums:** Act as computers for transmitting spells.
- **Kuduro / Batida:** Electronic music as modern sorcery; rhythms encode *Kisua*.
- **Mulemba Network:** Baobab trees serve as teleportation gates for the initiated.

## Factions
- **The *Mukixi*:** Awakened sorcerers without formal training. Protagonists.
- **The *Bakululu*:** Those who betrayed the pact; their *Kimbu* turns against them.
- **The *Muxiluanda*:** Lost children disconnected from culture; they fade unless named back.
- **The *Nganga Mukulu*:** The old masters trying to rebuild the sorcerous order.
- **The *Soba* Council:** Traditional chiefs who manage *Kixila* (taboo law).
