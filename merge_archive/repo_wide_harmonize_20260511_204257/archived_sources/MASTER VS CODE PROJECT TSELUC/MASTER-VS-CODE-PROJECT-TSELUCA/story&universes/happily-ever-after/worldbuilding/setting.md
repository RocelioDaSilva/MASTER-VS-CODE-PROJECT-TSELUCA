# Setting & Atmosphere

## Time
Present day (or could be set in the late 1990s/early 2000s to reflect the era of the events). The story spans several years, from the couple’s early relationship to the present.

## Locations
- **The City Where They Met:** A vibrant city where both had established lives before the split.
- **The Opportunity City:** The destination that tempted one partner away.
- **The Suburban Dream:** Their eventual home, a symbol of the American Dream—a house with a yard, good schools, and the illusion of safety.
- **The Party Location:** The secluded place where the assault occurred and later where they set up the frame.
- **Prison:** The facility where the hit took place; a bleak, corrupt environment.
- **The Present:** Their home, now under siege by the blackmailer.

## Atmosphere
- Tension beneath domestic tranquility.
- Noir elements: secrets, surveillance, paranoia.
- Moral ambiguity: the couple are both victims and perpetrators.
