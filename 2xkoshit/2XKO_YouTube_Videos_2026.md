## PAIRING KNOWLEDGE — EKKO & AHRI

> Wiki source: wiki.play2xko.com/en-us/Ekko | wiki.play2xko.com/en-us/Ahri
> Synergy rating (wiki): **Good** — "a very strong duo for people looking for raw aggression, strong pressure and mixup while not giving up mobility and neutral control."

---

### Character Profiles

**Ekko** — Archetype: Mixup | Health: 925
- Fast ground and air movement with built-in crossup tools
- Left/right and high/low mixup through Chronostrike afterimage mechanic
- Lower individual hit damage (optimals struggle to hit 450 solo); relies on meter or assists for bigger numbers
- Great team support: Chronostrike Assist is a massive neutral tool; Timewinder Assist enables time-freeze combo extensions
- Great synergy with Freestyle Fuse and 2X Assist Fuse
- Weakness: low health makes him vulnerable to Touch of Deaths (ToDs); higher complexity

**Ahri** — Archetype: Rushdown | Health: 925
- Defining feature: Air Dash (`j.66`), cancellable after frame 6 — enables all of her offense
- Top-tier mobility; can play both rushdown and keepaway depending on situation
- Strong mixup: `2S2 (Bounce!)` + air dash cancel → layered high/low/throw/crossup mixups
- Good lockdown assist (`4T Foxfire Rain` = +33 on block) and quick rush assist (`5T Spirit Rush`)
- Weakness: tied second-lowest health in game; below-average damage without high-execution combos; no dedicated defensive tool

### Synergy Analysis

Both characters share 925 HP, making this a **glass cannon double rushdown** team. The pairing excels because their tools address each other's weaknesses:

- **Ekko covers Ahri's neutral:** Chronostrike Assist (5T) is a massive threat that lets Ahri operate behind it safely. Ahri doesn't have strong dedicated anti-neutral tools on her own, but Ekko's dashing assist checks opponents trying to advance.
- **Ahri covers Ekko's gaps:** Temporal Slip (6S1) has a natural gap between hit 2 and 3 that can be parried. Ahri's Foxfire Rain Assist (+33 on block) covers this gap, forcing the opponent to stay blocking while Ekko runs Chronoswitch mix.
- **Chronoswitch cheat:** Ekko can Chronoswitch after throw or after Mega Timewinder (normally can't call assist/tag then). This means Ahri can be called to assist/tagged in at times opponents don't expect.
- **Handshake Tag tech:** After Ahri's Spirit Rush Assist is blocked, Ahri bounces into the air. Handshake Tag to Ahri here = she's immediately actionable with air dash → instant overhead pressure on the opponent who just blocked.
- **Foxfire Rain lockdown for Chronoswitch:** Ahri's `4T` at +33 on block pins the opponent while Ekko rewinds to either side → true left/right 50/50 that the opponent cannot parry out of cleanly.
- **Combined mobility:** Both characters have excellent movement options (Ekko's air hop + Phase Dive rolls, Ahri's air dash). The team can play blistering fast neutral and offense.
- **Damage concern:** Neither character solo-hits very hard. Both rely on longer combos with scaling. This team requires meter management to hit optimal numbers.

**The key weakness:** Both having 925 HP means any Touch of Death combo (ToD) either character is at risk. Sloppy defense or dropped blocks can be punished by death.

---

### Combo Theory

**Ekko on point:**
- Standard BnB route: `2L > 2M > 5H > 5S2 > (5S2~2S2 or 5S2~5S2)`
- With Ahri assist: call Foxfire Rain at end of blockstring → Chronoswitch left/right during lockdown
- For higher damage: use Chronobreak (wall bounce) → continue with `5S2` loops or call `5T` assist
- Clone Loop (advanced): `j.[H]` (charged) + afterimage + delayed `j.S1` Air Timewinder = high damage extension; can repeat with assist routing
- After throw: Chronoswitch (since Ekko can rewind post-throw) → call Ahri assist or tag in for immediate pressure
- Tag routing as Ekko assist: `Launch > immediate 66 (air hop) > j.S2 (first hit only) > vr.2S2` → then either `7S2` (limit strike) or `6S1` (corner carry/ult)

**Ahri on point:**
- Advanced routes almost always use `[Super 1]` (held Orb of Deception) — fire and return up to 2x for extended hits
- Core loop: cancel into `Spirit Rush (XS2)` → air actions → `j.2S1` OTG → continue
- With Ekko Chronostrike Assist on hit: easy confirm into Ahri's combo routes
- Ekko's Timewinder Assist (time-freeze) gives Ahri extra time to run more hits mid-combo
- Handshake Tag during `5S2 (Wild Flares)` → partner (Ekko) runs mixup while Ahri holds lockdown

**Meter spending priority:**
- Ahri: `[Super 1]` (Held Orb) in almost every advanced route
- Ekko: `Super 1` (Mega Timewinder) mid-combo → can super again or continue into tag launcher
- Avoid spending on `Ultimate (Charm)` for Ahri unless opportunity cost doesn't matter — Held Orb is almost always better

---

### Playstyle

**Neutral:** Ekko on point uses `5S1 (Timewinder)` with tag cancel to control the ground for free while Ahri handles jumping opponents from assist. Ahri on point uses Air Dash for high-speed rushdown approach. Both can fish for whiff punishes with `5M`.

**Offense:** The team opens with layered mixups:
- Ekko: `3L` (low) / `3M` (overhead) / `2S2` (Phase Dive crossup) / Replay left-right mix after Chronostrike
- Ahri: `2S2 (Bounce!)` → air dash cancel → double overhead / crossup / empty low / throw

**Defense:** Both characters lack strong defensive options. Rely on Ekko's Attempt #37 (Ultimate, reversal with invuln) for emergency defense. Ahri's 6-frame jab is her best interrupt option.

**Okizeme:** 
- Ekko: forward throw → Phase Dive (2S2) crossup → left/right 50/50 on wake-up
- Ahri: `j.2S2` knockdown → Stalking Dart (`2S1`) setup → meaty pressure

**Tag management:** Both can play point or anchor. Ekko tends to build meter for Ahri to spend on Held Orb routes. Ahri tends to generate neutral space Ekko can abuse with assists.

- High-damage, long-reaching attacks; best-in-class corner pressure and okizeme

### Synergy Analysis

This is an **opposite archetype pairing**: Ekko (fast/mixup) + Illaoi (bruiser/pressure/tentacles). Their tools compensate for each other's primary weaknesses:

- **Illaoi's tentacles stay on screen when she tags out:** Ekko comes in and fights with Illaoi's tentacles still active. This means Ekko can trigger tentacle follow-ups during his own combos and pressure sequences. Tentacles that Illaoi set up continue threatening the opponent even while she rests.
- **Ekko covers Illaoi's big weaknesses:** Harsh Lesson (6S2, her overhead) is parryable on reaction. Calling Ekko's Chronostrike Assist during Harsh Lesson's startup keeps the opponent in blockstun and covers the parry window. Illaoi's slow neutral is also covered by Ekko's fast Chronostrike Assist threatening from off-screen.
- **Illaoi's assists are among the best in the game:** Guiding Hand Assist (+34 on block) and Tentacle Smash Assist (+37 on block) give Ekko exceptional lockdown during his Chronoswitch left/right mix. The tentacle from Guiding Hand assist landing on hit is an additional bonus.
- **Charged 2S2 (Tracking From the Depths):** Illaoi can charge this move and it autofires when she tags out. This creates a delayed threat that catches opponent movement while Ekko is on point — adds unpredictability to Ekko's approach.
- **Corner synergy:** Illaoi's corner presence + Ekko's Phase Dive crossups + rewind left/rights = an extremely hard-to-defend corner trap. Illaoi pushes to corner, Ekko runs left/right while tentacle threatens, neither option is clean for the opponent.
- **Damage potential:** Illaoi hits much harder than Ekko individually. When Ekko converts into a tag launcher and Illaoi comes in near a tentacle, the damage is significantly higher. Illaoi's `2[H]` wall bounce and `5[H]` charged follow-ups scale well off existing combos.
- **Health disparity is an asset:** Illaoi's higher HP makes this team more durable than Ekko/Ahri. Ekko's lower HP means it's often smart to run Illaoi as point to absorb early pressure.

### Combo Theory

**Illaoi on point:**
- Core goal: get a tentacle on screen as fast as possible
- Standard combo: `2L > 2M > 5S2 (Guiding Hand) > 2H > j.L > j.M > j.H > j.S2 (Faith in Motion)` — spawns tentacle at end for okizeme
- Throw + tentacle → Throw Tentacle Follow Up → `2[H]` (wall bounce midscreen) or `5[H]` (corner) → continue with Ekko assist or tag
- Pressure string with gaps: `2L > dl.2L > dl.2M > 2S2` — gaps catch jumps and mashing; overhead during From the Depths if Ekko assist called during lows
- With Wrath of Nagakabouros (Super 2 buff): unlimited tentacles + auto-attack → mix freely for 6 seconds

**Ekko on point (with Illaoi assist):**
- Illaoi Guiding Hand Assist on hit spawns tentacle → Ekko can trigger tentacle follow-up by hitting opponent near it
- Standard Ekko route into tag launcher → Illaoi lands near tentacle → `5[H]` or `Throw Tentacle Follow Up` → higher damage than standard
- Chronoswitch after throw → tag to Illaoi who lands near previously placed tentacle = free combo extension

**Combo ender priority:**
- On knockdown: always try to end with a tentacle on screen for okizeme
- Illaoi's `2H` launcher as combo ender → tentacle stays, opponent forced to wake up under pressure
- Ekko's `5S2~2S2 (Time's Up!)` as tag route → Illaoi comes in, tentacle still active

### Playstyle

**Neutral:** Slower and more controlled than Ekko/Ahri. Illaoi uses `5S2 (Guiding Hand)` and `6S1 (Riptide)` to advance and establish tentacle presence. Ekko Assist covers the gaps in Illaoi's approach. Once tentacles are set, Illaoi's neutral becomes dramatically stronger.

**Offense:** Two distinct modes:
- Illaoi on point: raw hit damage + tentacle follow-ups; overhead/low mixup with `6S2` (overhead) and `2S2` / `2M` (lows); needs assist to make `6S2` safe
- Ekko on point: left/right Chronoswitch mixup backed by Illaoi's high-blockstun assists; Phase Dive crossup near placed tentacles

**Defense:** Illaoi's higher health means she can take more neutral damage. Test of Spirit (Ultimate) gives invuln reversal for emergency defense. Ekko has Attempt #37 (Ultimate, reversal).

**Okizeme:** This is the pairing's strongest phase:
- End combos with tentacle on screen → every option on wake-up becomes threatening:
  - Opponent gets up into tentacle range → `1S1 (Prayer in Stagnation)` grabs them from below
  - `4S1 (Prayer of Motion)` drags them to tentacle for crumple on counter hit
  - Call Ekko assist → crossup while tentacle is out
- `2[S2]` (charged From the Depths) set before tagging → autofires when Ekko is playing point

**Corner pressure:** Illaoi establishes corner position, places tentacles. Ekko tags in and runs Phase Dive crossups + Chronoswitch left/rights while tentacles auto-threaten. Opponent is guessing on multiple axes simultaneously.
